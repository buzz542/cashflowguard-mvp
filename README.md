# GuardConstruct

**Improve cash flow on every job.**

UK-first commercial risk review for small construction firms and freelancers (under 25 employees).

Free first-pass review of the payment traps that most often delay or reduce payment under English law.

> This is commercial risk identification only. It is **not legal advice**.

## Live site

https://cashflowguard-mvp.vercel.app

## Features in this version

- Simple email + password account
- First review free, then subscription gate
- Home / My Reviews / About tabs
- Saved review history (local to the device for now)
- Cleaner risk register with British English
- No duplicate disclaimer
- Focused landing page messaging around cash flow

## Environment variable required

`ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` – your Anthropic API key

## Cost

Typically 5p–20p per review using Claude Sonnet 5.
